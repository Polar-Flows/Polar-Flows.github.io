1. Introduction

Polar Flows ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices when you visit our website.

As data professionals who work with data every day, we understand the importance of information handling and believe privacy is of utmost importance. We recognize the responsibility that comes with handling information, which is why we have made a conscious decision to not collect any data through cookies, tracking technologies, or analytics tools on our website. Your privacy is not just a legal requirement for us - it's a fundamental principle we live by.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 No Automatic Data Collection

Our website does not use cookies, tracking technologies, or analytics tools. We do not automatically collect any personal information when you visit our website.

2.2 Information You Provide

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Contact us through our contact form
  • Send us an email directly

This information may include:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number)
  • Company name and job title
  • Project requirements and business information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Provide information about our services
  • Communicate with you about potential projects

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We only share your information:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal requirements
  • To protect our rights and safety

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect any personal information you provide to us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies or any tracking technologies. We do not collect analytics data, user behavior data, or any other information about your browsing activity.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about any personal data we may have
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent at any time

8. Data Retention

We retain any personal information you provide only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or as required by law.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Please refer to GitHub's privacy policy for information about their data practices. We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
